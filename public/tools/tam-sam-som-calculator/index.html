How to read the answer

The calculator multiplies your Total potential companies by your Average contract value in dollars to get TAM, multiplies TAM by your ICP percentage to get SAM, and multiplies SAM by your Year 1 market share target to get SOM and the number of Year 1 customers needed. If you leave ICP percentage or Year 1 market share target blank, the tool uses labelled example presets (30% ICP match and 3.0% Year 1 share) and warns you that the scenario does not establish attainable market share. Check What This Means for Your Business for the implied monthly deal pace and required pipeline (SOM × 3 at a 1-in-3 win rate).

What to do next

  • Founder: Replace any preset 30% ICP match rate or 3.0% Year 1 market share with your own measured percentages and cross-reference your total company count across two dated sources.
  • CEO: Compare the Deals Needed and Monthly Target under What This Means for Your Business against the number of deals your team actually closed last year before putting the Investor-Ready Summary into a board or fundraising deck.
  • CMO: Validate the ICP percentage against your CRM account analysis so SAM reflects companies that genuinely have the problem and budget you serve.
  • CRO: Check whether your sales team has the rep capacity and win rate to generate and close the Pipeline Required (SOM × 3) at your stated average contract value.
  • Demand generation head: Take the validated SAM segment definition into the Lookalike Signal Generator to build your reachable account and lead lists for Year 1.

Why come back

Come back whenever your average contract value shifts, you verify a sharper account count from dated industry records, or you need a separate bottom-up sizing for a new market segment or geography. It runs the same fixed rules every time in your browser or in your own assistant, and your inputs are not stored or sent to an AI model.

How do I calculate TAM bottom up?

Count the companies that could buy, multiply by the contract value you really win, then narrow to the share that fits your ICP and the share you can win in year 1. That is TAM, then SAM, then SOM.

Helix does not rank vendors or name software to buy for market sizing. Run this free calculator for a first number, check the company count against two dated sources and the contract value against your last closed deals, or book 45 minutes.
