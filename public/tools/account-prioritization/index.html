Account Prioritization

Rank and prioritize your accounts

Give it your accounts with fit, intent, relationship and timing scores. Get a ranked list, a tier for each account and the next action for each one.

Try it with an exampleUse your own details

Last updated 29 September 2026

Run Account Prioritization

Every field is optional. You can add: Accounts to rank, Your own weights in percent.

One entry per account. Scores run from 0 to 100; timing is now, soon, later or unknown. For example: [{"name": "Clinic Group A", "fit_score": 80, "intent_signals": 60, "relationship": 40, "timing": "soon"}]

Leave it empty to use the defaults (fit 40, intent 30, relationship 15, timing 15). For example: {"fit": 50, "intent": 30, "relationship": 10, "timing": 10}

Who is Account Prioritization not ideal for?

  • Teams looking for a list of companies to target: the tools describe and score profiles from your data but find no companies.
  • Market sizing without your own numbers: TAM, SAM and SOM are built from the figures you enter; an ICP match rate or market share you leave out is filled with a labelled example (30% and 3%).

How do I use Account Prioritization from Claude?

Add ICP Intelligence once (see Use it in Claude or ChatGPT), then ask in plain words. A prompt built from the tested example:

Use ICP Intelligence's Account Prioritization tool. accounts: Clinic Group A (fit score 80, intent signals 60, relationship 40, timing soon); Clinic Group B (fit score 55, intent signals 90, relationship 70, timing now).

How does Account Prioritization work?

No sign-in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run Account Prioritization.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the ICP Intelligence code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
