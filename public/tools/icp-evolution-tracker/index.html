How to read the answer

The tracker compares your Current ICP text against your Recent wins, Recent losses, and Market changes and outputs Win/Loss Pattern Analysis (Candidate addition and Candidate disqualifier), Market Change Impact, Your Current ICP, Part by Part, a Draft ICP for the period, and What to pull from your CRM. Every suggested addition or disqualifier is explicitly marked as a hypothesis to test against your CRM win rates, ACV, sales cycle, and churn before you rewrite your official ICP.

What to do next

  • Founder: Check What to pull from your CRM before changing strategy: compare win rate, ACV, sales cycle, and churn between your core ICP segment and the Add on trial segment.
  • CEO: Keep your core segment unchanged until the CRM numbers prove the Candidate addition closes faster, larger, or retains better than your average.
  • CMO: Test messaging for the Add on trial segment in a bounded campaign while updating qualification questions to reflect the Market Change Impact.
  • CRO: Add the Qualify out on trial criteria and the sector loss reasons to your CRM disqualification tags so reps stop burning cycles on unwinnable deals.
  • Demand generation head: Run a small pilot campaign for the Add on trial segment and exclude the Candidate disqualifier profile from paid targeting.

Why come back

Run this tool at every quarterly business review with that quarter's won deals, lost deals, and market shifts to decide what to keep, add on trial, or qualify out next quarter. It runs the same fixed rules every time in your browser or in your own assistant, and your inputs are not stored or sent to an AI model.

How does the ICP Evolution Tracker tool work?

You do not need to sign in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run ICP Evolution Tracker.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the ICP Intelligence code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
