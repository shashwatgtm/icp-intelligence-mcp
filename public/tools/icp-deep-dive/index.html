How do I find ICP patterns from our best B2B customers and CRM data?

Enter your best customers in ICP Deep Dive as a plain-text description or as structured customer rows with name, industry, size, ACV, and champion title, plus optional sales cycle days, buying trigger, and tech stack. The tool extracts shared patterns across industry, company size, deal economics (average ACV, ACV range, and average sales cycle), tech stack signals, champion roles, and buying triggers, listing tied values when there is no single leader and adding sector notes.

How to read the answer

When you enter a plain-text description of your best customers, the tool extracts Detected Patterns (Likely Company Size, Likely Sector, Likely Stage, usual buyer roles, and sector metrics) and prompts you under Recommendations to supply structured customer rows (name, industry, size, ACV, champion title, plus optional sales cycle, buying trigger, and tech stack). When you enter structured customer rows, it calculates the shared patterns across your list. It never looks up external companies; it only summarizes patterns in the data you provide and adds sector notes where your product category is clear.

What to do next

  • Founder: Export your top 10 to 20 closed-won customers from your CRM in the 5-column format (name, industry, size, ACV, champion title) and re-run the tool to replace text impressions with hard counts.
  • CEO: Use the Detected Patterns and Sector notes to align leadership on which company size and buyer role represent your core market today.
  • CMO: Check your website copy and case studies against the Words this sector's buyers use and A proof point that lands printed in the sector notes.
  • CRO: Focus outbound prospecting on the company size range and champion title that appear most often in your best customer rows.
  • Demand generation head: Use the detected company size, sector, and buyer roles to tighten audience filters on LinkedIn and in your contact database.

Why come back

Come back as soon as you pull your structured CRM export (including sales cycle days, buying triggers, and tech stack) to get a data-backed pattern analysis, and re-run it every six months as new customers close. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.

How does the ICP Deep Dive tool work?

You do not need to sign in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run ICP Deep Dive.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the ICP Intelligence code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
