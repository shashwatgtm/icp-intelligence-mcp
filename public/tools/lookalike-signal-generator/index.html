How to read the answer

This tool generates copy-and-paste targeting criteria and search strings from your inputs (Industries, Company sizes, Locations, Funding stages, ICP technographics, Champion titles, and Buying triggers); it does not look up or export actual company or contact records. It outputs Company and lead search criteria (including a Boolean Search String), Advertising audience criteria, Intent criteria (in JSON format), Contact criteria, Buying Trigger Signals, and an Implementation Checklist. If your size labels are not explicit employee headcounts, a top note reminds you to supply employee counts before building headcount filters.

What to do next

  • Founder: Add your Product category (what you sell) if you left it blank and re-run the tool so the Custom Intent Audiences section includes product search keywords alongside technographic integration keywords.
  • CEO: Check the ICP Summary to ensure your outbound and paid teams are targeting the exact industries, geographies, and champion titles where you already win.
  • CMO: Use the Advertising audience criteria and Intent criteria JSON block to configure your custom intent topics, competitor website retargeting, and account fit model.
  • CRO: Have SDRs paste the Company Search, Lead Search, and Boolean Search String into your sales prospecting tool, save the search with weekly alerts, and monitor the Buying Trigger Signals.
  • Demand generation head: Work through the four Implementation Checklist blocks, verifying a 10% sample of exported contacts for data quality before launching outreach sequences.

Why come back

Run the generator again whenever you add a new champion title, discover a new technographic signal or buying trigger from customer interviews, or expand into a new geography or industry. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.

How does the Lookalike Signal Generator tool work?

You do not need to sign in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.
