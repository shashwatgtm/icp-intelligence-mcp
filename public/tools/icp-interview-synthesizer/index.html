How to read the answer

The synthesizer counts how often each pain point, buying trigger, post-purchase value, and champion role appears across the structured interview lines you entered (customer | role | pain points | buying triggers | value realized | key quotes). It prints Pattern Analysis with exact frequencies (for example, in 1 of 2 interviews (50%)), Key Quotes, ICP Refinements from Interviews, Discovery Questions to Add, and an Interview Template for Next Round. Under Value Realized (Post-Purchase), it warns you to treat reported outcomes as claims to verify with source, scope, and date before leading with them in public copy.

What to do next

  • Founder: Look at the frequency counts under Pattern Analysis: if only 2 interviews are entered and every pattern sits at 50%, run 5 to 8 more interviews before locking your ICP.
  • CEO: Verify the Value Realized claims against customer records and obtain source, scope, and date before putting any outcome figure into your pitch deck.
  • CMO: Update your Pain messaging and Value messaging with the top recurring customer phrases and Key Quotes once a clear majority pattern emerges.
  • CRO: Add the Discovery Questions to Add and Sector questions to your sales discovery playbook so reps probe the exact pains customers named.
  • Demand generation head: Time outbound outreach around the Top Buying Triggers (such as a new CFO hire or an audit finding) and target the top Champion Roles.

Why come back

Come back after every new batch of 3 to 5 customer or win/loss interviews, append the new pipe-separated notes to your list, and re-run the synthesizer to see which pain points and triggers rise to the top.

How does the ICP Interview Synthesizer tool work?

You do not need to sign in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run ICP Interview Synthesizer.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the ICP Intelligence code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
