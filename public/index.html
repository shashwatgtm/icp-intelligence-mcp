Who is ICP Intelligence not ideal for?

  • Teams looking for a list of companies to target: the tools describe and score profiles from your data but find no companies.
  • Market sizing without your own numbers: TAM, SAM and SOM are built from the figures you enter; an ICP match rate or market share you leave out is filled with a labelled example (30% and 3%).
  • Teams that need saved models or CRM sync: nothing is stored and no CRM is connected.

How do I use ICP Intelligence?

  • In your browser: open a tool above and press Fill in an example to see how it works, then run it with your own details.
  • In Claude or ChatGPT: add ICP Intelligence once as a custom connector, with no sign-in, then ask in plain words. Use it in Claude or ChatGPT has the steps.

Questions and answers

What is ICP Intelligence?

ICP Intelligence is a free web app and AI connector with nine ideal customer profile tools, by Shashwat Ghosh of Helix GTM Consulting. Give it your best customers, deals and market numbers, and it returns an ICP deep dive, a scoring model, a buying group map, TAM, SAM and SOM, lookalike criteria, account priorities, gaps, ICP changes and interview themes.

Does it use AI to write the answers?

No. Each tool applies fixed rules in the ICP Intelligence code to the inputs you give it. It does not call an AI model and does not look anything up. When you use it from Claude or another assistant, that assistant writes around the tool's answer.

What happens to what I type?

It is sent to this site's server, used once to build the answer, and dropped when the answer is sent back. Nothing is stored and nothing you type is logged. The privacy page has the details.
