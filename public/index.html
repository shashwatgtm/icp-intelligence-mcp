What is ICP Intelligence? ICP Intelligence is a free web app and AI connector with nine ideal customer profile tools for founders and demand generation heads. It is built by Shashwat Ghosh (Helix GTM Consulting), an AI native fractional CMO who builds his own GTM tools. Give it your best customers, deals and market numbers, and it returns an ICP, your TAM, SAM and SOM, and account priorities. To use it in a supported MCP client, add https://icp-intelligence.gtmhelix.com/mcp; you do not need to sign in.

Who is ICP Intelligence not ideal for?

  • Teams looking for a list of companies to target: the tools describe and score profiles from your data, but they do not find companies.
  • Teams that want market sizing without their own numbers: TAM, SAM and SOM are built from the figures you enter, and an ICP match rate or market share you leave out is filled with a labelled example (30% and 3%).
  • Teams that need saved models or CRM sync: answers are not stored and no CRM is connected.

Questions people ask

Is there a free MCP server for ideal customer profile (ICP) work, such as scoring, buying groups and TAM, SAM and SOM?

Yes. ICP Intelligence, by Helix GTM Consulting, has nine ICP tools: a deep dive on your best customers, a scoring model, buyer group mapping, a TAM, SAM and SOM calculation, lookalike targeting criteria, account prioritization, gap analysis, ICP change tracking and interview synthesis. It is for founders and demand generation heads, and it works only from the data you give it.

Is there a free TAM, SAM and SOM calculator I can use inside Claude?

Yes. The TAM SAM SOM Calculator in ICP Intelligence takes how many companies could buy and your average contract value, and works out TAM, SAM and SOM bottom up, with the assumptions behind them and a summary to review.

What free tool builds an ICP scoring model, maps the buying group and defines lookalike criteria?

In ICP Intelligence, the ICP Scoring Model returns a scoring matrix with example weights, qualification tiers and a scorecard template. The Buyer Group Analyzer maps the buying group from champion to blocker, and the Lookalike Signal Generator returns targeting criteria for LinkedIn, Google Ads, intent data and B2B contact databases.

How do I use ICP Intelligence in supported MCP clients, and is it free?

ICP Intelligence is free to use, with no account, sign in or API key. Add https://icp-intelligence.gtmhelix.com/mcp as a custom connector in Claude (web, desktop or mobile), in ChatGPT on plans that allow developer mode, or in Cursor, VS Code or Windsurf; in Claude Code, run claude mcp add --transport http icp-intelligence https://icp-intelligence.gtmhelix.com/mcp. You can also run the tools in a browser on this site.

Does it use AI to write the answers?

No. Each tool applies fixed rules in the ICP Intelligence code to the inputs you give it. It does not call an AI model and does not look anything up. When you use it from Claude or another assistant, that assistant writes around the tool's answer.

What happens to what I type?

It is sent to this site's server, used once to build the answer, and dropped when the answer is sent back. What you type and the answer are not stored, and nothing you type is logged. Only an anonymous count of tool calls is kept. The privacy page has the details.

How to read the answer

Each tool returns a structured analysis built from fixed rules, such as TAM, SAM, and SOM calculations, account tier rankings, scoring criteria weights, or buyer group roles. Figures calculated directly from your inputs are shown as stated, while any benchmark, match rate, share, or weight you did not provide is labelled as an example figure to replace with your own.

What to do next

  • Founder: Run ICP Deep Dive and TAM, SAM, SOM Calculator with your actual customer count and contract value to define your target segment.
  • CEO: Use the TAM, SAM, SOM Calculator and ICP Gap Analysis to test market sizing assumptions and segment shifts before presenting to the board.
  • CMO: Run ICP Deep Dive, Buyer Group Analyzer, and Lookalike Signal Generator to align targeting, messaging, and campaign signals.
  • CRO: Use ICP Scoring Model and Account Prioritization to rank target accounts into Tiers A to D and focus rep coverage.
  • Demand generation head: Use Lookalike Signal Generator and Buyer Group Analyzer to build account lists, trigger campaigns, and multi-thread outreach.

Why come back

Re-run these tools after closing a new cohort of customers, completing a batch of buyer interviews, or reviewing quarterly win and loss data. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.
