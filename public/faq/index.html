ICP Intelligence: questions and answers

Short answers you can check against the code and the privacy policy.

By Shashwat Ghosh, Co-Founder and Fractional CMO, Helix GTM Consulting · Last updated 28 September 2026

How do I use ICP Intelligence?

  • In your browser: open a tool above and press Fill in an example to see how it works, then run it with your own details.
  • In Claude or ChatGPT: add ICP Intelligence once as a custom connector, with no sign-in, then ask in plain words. Use it in Claude or ChatGPT has the steps.

Cost, privacy and how it works

What is ICP Intelligence?

ICP Intelligence is a free web app and AI connector with nine ideal customer profile tools, by Shashwat Ghosh of Helix GTM Consulting. Give it your best customers, deals and market numbers, and it returns an ICP deep dive, a scoring model, a buying group map, TAM, SAM and SOM, lookalike criteria, account priorities, gaps, ICP changes and interview themes.

Is ICP Intelligence free, and do I need an account?

It is free, and there is no account or sign-in. Use the tools in your browser here, or add them to Claude or ChatGPT as a connector.

Does it use AI to write the answers?

No. Each tool applies fixed rules in the ICP Intelligence code to the inputs you give it. It does not call an AI model and does not look anything up. When you use it from Claude or another assistant, that assistant writes around the tool's answer.

What happens to what I type?

It is sent to this site's server, used once to build the answer, and dropped when the answer is sent back. Nothing is stored and nothing you type is logged. The privacy page has the details.

How do I use ICP Intelligence in Claude?

In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors, select + and then Add custom connector. Name it ICP Intelligence, paste https://icp-intelligence.gtmhelix.com/mcp and choose no sign-in. The page Use it in Claude or ChatGPT covers Claude Code, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code and Windsurf too.

Why do some answers look like templates?

Several tools return a structured plan with [placeholders] where your inputs did not give a fact. When an answer shows a benchmark, target, rate, weight or other figure you did not supply, it is labelled "Example figure: replace with your own". Figures calculated from your inputs are not labelled. Suggested lengths, timings and counts are marked as suggestions. Replace every example and placeholder before you use the result.

Can I call the tools from my own code?

Yes. Send POST https://icp-intelligence.gtmhelix.com/api/tools/<tool name> with a JSON body {"input": {...}}. The Docs page lists every field. Please keep to the rate limit of 30 requests per minute.

Is the source code public?

Yes, at https://github.com/shashwatgtm/icp-intelligence-mcp under the MIT licence. The npm package @shashwatgtmalpha/icp-intelligence-mcp runs these tools locally; it can lag behind this site until a new version is published.

Who is ICP Intelligence not ideal for?

Teams looking for a list of companies to target: the tools describe and score profiles from your data but find no companies. Market sizing without your own numbers: TAM, SAM and SOM are built from the figures you enter; an ICP match rate or market share you leave out is filled with a labelled example (30% and 3%). Teams that need saved models or CRM sync: nothing is stored and no CRM is connected.

How do I report a problem?

Email shashwat@gtmhelix.com or open an issue at https://github.com/shashwatgtm/icp-intelligence-mcp/issues.
